# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Behavior that contributes to a positive environment

* **Demonstrate empathy and kindness** toward other community members
* **Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences** — technical disagreements are healthy; personal attacks are not
* **Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback** — focus on the code/design, not the person
* **Accept responsibility** for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from the experience
* **Focus on what is best for the community and the project** — not just for individuals or specific use cases

### Behavior that is unacceptable

* **Harassment** — trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment
* **Discrimination** — publishing others' private information without consent, doxxing, or discriminatory jokes
* **Sexual attention** — unwelcome sexual advances, sexualized language/imagery, or sexual attention
* **Disruption** — sustained disruption of discussions, deliberate derailment of technical conversations, spam
* **Bad-faith contributions** — PRs/issues intended to waste maintainer time, demonstrate superiority, or push personal agendas without project alignment

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub Issues, PRs, Discussions, Discord if applicable) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at **conduct@freellm.hasit.in**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Behavior | Consequence |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| 1. Correction | Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community | Private written warning; public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A violation through a single incident or series of actions | Temporary ban from community spaces (1-30 days); no interaction with maintainers during period |
| 3. Temporary Ban | A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior | Ban from all community spaces for a specified period (30-365 days) |
| 4. Permanent Ban | A pattern of violation of community standards, including harassment or discrimination toward an individual or group | Permanent ban from all community spaces |
